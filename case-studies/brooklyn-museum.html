UX Design Sprint

Brooklyn Museum — Data-fed App Experience

Designing a mobile experience that complements the museum visit with real-time data on exhibits, wait times, and personalized guidance.

Hero visual — app screens / phone mockups

Overview

We were tasked with the brief to pick a cultural institution and reimagine what a data-centered experience might be for an institution that does not traditionally use data visualization as a core part of their immersive experience.

The Solution

Our goal was to enable museum goers and visitors to make the most of their time at the Brooklyn Museum. We identified several key features we felt would be valuable and welcomed in this design context.
